## 4. The monetization math (why long-form = fastest)

**YouTube Partner Program (long-form path):** 1,000 subscribers **+ 4,000 valid
public watch hours** in 12 months.

- 4,000 hours = **240,000 minutes**.
- An emotional story ≈ **30–40 min**. At a modest 35% average view duration on a
  35-min video ≈ **12 min watched per view**.
- 240,000 ÷ 12 ≈ **~20,000 views** to clear the watch-hour bar.
- Dreamline's top video did **1.75M views** — that single upload is ~50–80× the
  entire requirement.

➡️ **Contrast with Shorts** (needs 10M Shorts views in 90 days) or 3-min
folktales (you'd need ~5× more views for the same watch hours). **Long-form
emotional stories are the single fastest route to the 4,000-hour bar.**

## 5. Your consistency system (the real fix)

You didn't fail at African stories — you failed at a format that demanded too
many uploads. Switch to a **low-volume, batch-produced** system:

- **Cadence:** 1 video/week (or even 2/month). That's it.
- **Batch days:** Write 4 scripts in one sitting → generate all voiceovers →
  generate visuals → edit. Produce a month of content in 2–3 focused days.
- **Repeatable template:** Same title formula, same intro hook, same voice,
  same thumbnail style. Consistency of *format* > consistency of *frequency*.
- **Series, not one-offs:** "The Wife They…", "The Bride He…", "She Was Only
  a Maid…". A repeatable emotional premise removes the "what do I make?" friction
  that kills consistency.

**Winning title pattern (proven, from the data):**
`[Wronged woman] + [betrayal] … [twist/justice]`
e.g. "The Wife They Threw Out", "He Left Her at the Altar — Then Learned Who She Really Was".

**Winning hook pattern (first line):** state the injustice in one emotional
sentence — _"She was mocked for being childless, then thrown out the day her
husband was declared dead."_

## 6. Tools to find & validate niches (what you asked for)

**Niche research / trend discovery**
- **vidIQ** — keyword scores, "Daily Ideas", outlier/velocity detection. (They now even expose an MCP — see `vidIQ.com/MCP`.)
- **TubeBuddy** — keyword explorer + competitor tags.
- **1of10 (1of10.com)** — finds "outlier" videos doing way above a channel's average. Best tool for spotting a format that's breaking out early.
- **Google Trends** — free; validate whether a topic is rising or fading.
- **Social Blade / Playboard** — track a competitor's growth *rate* and estimated earnings; confirm a channel is genuinely accelerating (like Dreamline).
- **This YouTube MCP** — exactly what I just used: `searchVideos`, `getChannelStatistics`, `getChannelTopVideos`, `getVideoComments`, `getTranscripts`. Great for pulling real subs/views/velocity + audience sentiment on demand.

**Production (near-zero cost, from the top creators' own stacks)**
- **Scripts:** ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs or Google AI Studio — pick a *custom* voice, not the default preset, so you don't sound like everyone else.
- **Visuals:** Nano Banana (Gemini image), Whisk, Kling, Grok, Higgsfield/Seedance for motion.
- **Editing:** CapCut (most of it runs on your phone).

**⚠️ Monetization safety (so you don't get demonetized):** write your OWN
scripts, record/generate your OWN narration, and never re-upload someone else's
footage or sermon. If you use AI voice/images, add a "made with AI" note in the
description. YouTube flags reused/re-uploaded content — original writing + real
editing is what keeps you monetizable.
